# Description

## zh

面向法国行政与专业服务的技能合集，包含会计、税务、审计、公证和物业管理角色。

## en

A collection for French bureaucracy and professional services, including accounting, tax, audit, notarial, and property-management roles.

# Long Description

## zh

仓库用法语专业角色整理常见行政工作流，适合解释流程和准备材料清单。它不构成法律、税务或会计意见，法规和个案结论必须由法国合格专业人士核验。

## en

The repository organizes French administrative workflows around specialist roles and can explain processes or prepare document lists. It is not legal, tax, or accounting advice; qualified French professionals must verify rules and case-specific conclusions.

## en
