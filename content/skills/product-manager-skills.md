## en

# Long Description

## zh

仓库以标准 SKILL.md 提供可安装工作流。框架不能替代用户证据；路线图、优先级和对外承诺必须由产品负责人批准。

## en

The repository provides installable workflows through standard SKILL.md files. Frameworks do not replace user evidence; product owners must approve roadmaps, priorities, and external commitments.

# Usage

## zh

先在非生产环境选择匹配任务的技能，并限定输入与权限。框架不能替代用户证据；路线图、优先级和对外承诺必须由产品负责人批准。

## en

Start in a non-production environment with task-matched skills and bounded inputs and permissions. Frameworks do not replace user evidence; product owners must approve roadmaps, priorities, and external commitments.

# Workflow

## en

3. Review results before applying or publishing
