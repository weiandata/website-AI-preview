综合营销技能合集，为代理提供策略、增长、内容和营销活动执行方面的可复用工作流。

## en

# Long Description

## zh

该仓库包含多项独立营销技能，可通过 Skills CLI 安装到多种代理客户端。它适合从市场信息、客户研究和品牌约束出发，生成策略分析与执行素材。营销结论应尽量由真实客户数据支持，任何对外文案、自动触达或广告支出都需要人工审批并遵守隐私和平台政策。

## en

The repository contains many independent marketing skills installable through the Skills CLI across several agent clients. It can turn market information, customer research, and brand constraints into strategic analysis and execution assets. Ground marketing conclusions in real customer data where possible, and require human approval for external copy, automated outreach, or advertising spend while respecting privacy and platform policies.

## en

After installation, select the most relevant skill and provide target customers, product facts, brand voice, channels, budget, and success metrics. Route generated materials through factual, legal, privacy, and brand review before an authorized person publishes or spends.

# FAQ

## Question 1

### question.zh

可以让代理自动发送营销内容吗？

### question.en

Can the agent automatically send marketing content?

### answer.zh

不应在无人审核时直接发送。自动触达涉及品牌、隐私、退订、平台政策和预算风险，需要明确授权与人工审批。

### answer.en

It should not send content without review. Automated outreach carries brand, privacy, unsubscribe, platform-policy, and budget risks, so it needs explicit authorization and human approval.
