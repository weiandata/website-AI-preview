## en

A music-creation skill collection for Claude Code, providing structured agent guidance for composition, production, and audio workflows.

# Long Description

## zh

仓库以 Claude 插件形式组织多个音乐技能，并提供市场安装与初始化步骤。它适合辅助整理创作方向、音乐结构和制作流程，而不是替代音乐人的听觉判断。使用外部音频工具、模型或素材时，需要自行确认软件环境、授权条款和作品版权。

## en

The repository organizes multiple music skills as a Claude plugin and documents marketplace installation and setup. It helps shape creative direction, musical structure, and production workflow rather than replacing a musician's listening judgment. When external audio tools, models, or assets are involved, verify the software environment, licensing terms, and rights in the work.

# Usage

## zh

安装并按仓库说明完成配置后，提供风格参考、乐器配置、时长、结构和交付格式。把代理产出当作创作与制作建议，最终通过监听、编辑和导出测试确认结果。

## en

After installation and repository setup, provide style references, instrumentation, duration, structure, and delivery format. Treat agent output as creative and production guidance, then confirm it through listening, editing, and export tests.
