## en

Academic workflow skills spanning research, writing, review, revision, and finalization.

# Long Description

## zh

仓库提供深度研究、论文撰写、评审和完整流水线技能，帮助代理组织研究过程。模型可能产生错误引用或过度结论，所有来源、数据和论证都需研究者核验。

## en

The repository provides deep-research, paper-writing, review, and full-pipeline skills. Models can produce incorrect citations or overstate conclusions, so researchers must verify every source, dataset, and argument.

# Usage

## zh

提供研究问题、方法、已核实来源和引用规范。要求区分证据与推断，并在原始来源中逐条核对引用。

## en

Provide the research question, method, verified sources, and citation style. Require separation of evidence from inference and check every citation against its original source.

# Workflow

## zh

1. 定义问题与证据标准
2. 研究、写作并独立评审
3. 核对来源后定稿

## en

1. Define the question and evidence standard
2. Research, write, and review independently
3. Verify sources before finalizing

# FAQ

## Question 1

### question.zh

生成的引用可以直接使用吗？

### question.en

Can generated citations be used directly?

### answer.zh

不能。必须回到原始出版物确认作者、题名、年份、页码和结论。

### answer.en

No. Confirm authors, title, year, pages, and claims in the original publication.
