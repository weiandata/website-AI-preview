## en

# Long Description

## zh

## en

The repository divides growth-marketing work into selectable skills for a consistent research-to-execution process. Public content, ad budgets, and customer data still require brand, privacy, and approval controls.

# Usage

## zh

提供目标客户、产品事实、渠道、基线数据与指标。发布或花费预算前，由负责人核查事实、隐私和品牌一致性。

## en

Provide target customers, product facts, channels, baseline data, and metrics. Before publishing or spending, have an owner review facts, privacy, and brand consistency.

# Workflow

3. 审批、执行并衡量结果

## en

1. Select a marketing skill and metric
2. Provide customer evidence and constraints
3. Approve, execute, and measure results
