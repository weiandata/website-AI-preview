# Description

## zh

12 个技能，帮你用自己的语气写 LinkedIn 帖子、评论和回复，发布前等待你确认。

## en

Twelve skills that write LinkedIn posts, comments, and replies in your voice, waiting for your approval before publishing.

# Long Description

## zh

这组技能用来起草内容、去掉 AI 写作痕迹，并在发布前等待确认，不要求写代码。它可以用于 Claude Code、Codex 和 claude.ai 网页版。同一团队还有面向 X、Instagram、YouTube 的同类技能包。使用时请遵守 LinkedIn 平台规则，并亲自检查以你名义发布的内容。

## en

These skills draft content, strip AI tells, and wait for approval before publishing, with no coding needed. They work in Claude Code, Codex, and claude.ai on the web. The same team ships similar bundles for X, Instagram, and YouTube. Follow LinkedIn's platform rules and check anything published in your name.

# Usage

## zh

安装后告诉代理要写的主题，并提供你的写作样本以匹配语气。Codex 使用 codex plugin 命令安装，claude.ai 用户可在 Customize 中添加。发布前逐条检查事实和语气。

## en

After installing, give the agent a topic and writing samples to match your voice. Codex installs with the codex plugin commands, and claude.ai users add it under Customize. Check facts and tone in every draft before publishing.

# Workflow

2. 提供写作样本
3. 说明主题和目标读者
4. 审阅并修改草稿
5. 确认后发布

## en

1. Install the skills
2. Provide writing samples
3. State the topic and audience
4. Review and edit the draft
5. Approve and publish
