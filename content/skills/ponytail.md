## en

# Long Description

## zh

仓库把资深工程师的精简思维拆成多个 SKILL.md，帮助质疑不必要实现并发现删除或简化机会。 少写代码不等于忽略需求；删除或重构前必须确认行为、调用方、测试和回滚方案。

## en

The repository splits a senior-engineering simplification mindset into SKILL.md files that challenge unnecessary implementation and surface removal opportunities. Writing less code does not mean skipping requirements; verify behavior, callers, tests, and rollback before deletion or refactoring.
