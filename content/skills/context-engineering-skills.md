## en

A context-engineering collection for production agents, covering compression, memory, multi-agent architectures, and advanced evaluation.

# Long Description

## zh

仓库包含从基础上下文管理到复杂代理系统的多个技能和示例。它帮助团队设计信息流与评估方法，但具体性能必须通过真实任务、成本和质量指标验证。

The repository includes skills and examples from basic context management to complex agent systems. It helps design information flow and evaluation, but performance must be tested on real tasks, cost, and quality metrics.
